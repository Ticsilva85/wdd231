About me

My name is Tiago Cavalcante, and I am an English teacher with a background in Languages and a strong interest in technology and software development. I hold a degree in Portuguese and English Languages and a postgraduate specialization in Innovative Practices in Bilingual Education. My experience in education has helped me develop strong communication, organization, problem-solving, and interpersonal skills.

After building my professional experience in education, I am now returning to the technology field and focusing on developing my career as a software developer, starting with front-end development. I have been studying HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, Git, and GitHub, while improving my knowledge of programming logic, responsive web design, and user experience.

I believe my background in education is an important advantage in this new career path. Teaching has taught me how to understand people's needs, explain complex ideas clearly, adapt to different situations, and continuously learn. I want to combine these skills with my technical knowledge to create useful, accessible, and user-friendly digital solutions.

My goal is to continue growing as a developer, build real-world projects, and gradually expand my knowledge into back-end development. I see this career transition as an opportunity to bring together my experience in education, communication, and technology while continuing to learn and contribute to meaningful projects.
