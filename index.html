About me

My name is Tiago Cavalcante, and I am an English teacher with a degree in Portuguese and English Languages and a postgraduate specialization in Bilingual Education. My experience in education has developed my communication, organization, and problem-solving skills.

I am now transitioning into software development, focusing on front-end development. I have been studying HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, Git, and GitHub, while developing my skills in programming logic and responsive web design.

My goal is to grow as a developer, build real-world projects, and eventually expand my knowledge into back-end development. I hope to combine my experience in education with technology to create useful and user-friendly digital solutions.
